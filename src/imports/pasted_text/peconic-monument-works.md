LEGACY / ABOUT STRIP
From a marker to a mausoleum
Established in 1842, Peconic Monument Works has been specializing in memorials at the same Riverhead location for over 172 years. We strive to offer superior customer service to our families, while providing the highest quality memorials that truly depict your vision. From a simple flat marker to an elaborate mausoleum, we are your full service memorial shop. We also specialize in cemetery lettering, repair work, and cleaning services. Our goal in working with each family is to make the process of designing a memorial as easy and encouraging as possible. We have a large indoor display of memorials to help families see some of the size, design, and finish options available. Although we have a variety of memorials on hand, please know that each memorial is custom made. Every memorial is created by the hands of skilled craftsmen who cut and personalize each memorial and ultimately create a cherished representation of a loved one's life. A memorial is a reflection of personal taste, but it is also the way one will be remembered for generations to come. It can even be the only permanent purchase a family makes in their lifetime.

MAIN CONTENT — LEFT COLUMN
Four Generations. One Standard.
— Section label: OUR HERITAGE —
Founded in 1842 as Frank H. Hill Peconic Monument Works has stood on Griffing Avenue in Riverhead through nearly two centuries of families and change. Since 1982, the Warner family has continued this tradition — today led by Hollis, his son John, and daughter-in-law Ashley.
Each memorial is made entirely by hand. John — a third-generation stone carver — learned by watching his father after school, tracing letters onto rubber stencils, cutting each one carefully, and using a pneumatic hammer to chisel the stone. The methods are time-honored. The care is absolute.
We have worked with thousands of families and organizations across Long Island, using only the highest quality stone — ensuring the monument you purchase will remain beautiful for generations to come.

PAGE BANNER
Frequently Asked Questions
We’re here to help guide you through the process.
Serving all of Long Island with custom memorials, restoration, and cemetery services.


Q1: How long does it take to make a headstone?
Typically 6–8 weeks from the time your design is approved, though timing can vary based on cemetery requirements and stone availability. We will keep you informed every step of the way.
Q2: What types of stone do you work with?
We work primarily with granite, as it is the most durable and weather-resistant stone available. We also offer marble and limestone for select applications, and bronze for flat markers and plaques.
Q3: Can you add lettering to an existing monument?
Yes — cemetery lettering is one of our most common services. We add names, dates, and inscriptions to existing headstones, working directly at the cemetery site.
Q4: Do you handle installation at the cemetery?
Yes. We coordinate all aspects of installation and work directly with each cemetery to ensure full compliance with their regulations. You don’t have to worry about any of that.
Q5: Can I include a photograph on the monument?
Yes. We offer laser-etched portrait engraving on granite, which produces a detailed, permanent likeness. We can work from a clear photograph to create a lasting image. We also work with porcelain photos if color is a priority.
Q6: What artwork and symbols can be included?
We can engrave virtually any symbol or image — from religious symbols and military insignia to custom artwork, flowers, landscapes, and personal motifs that reflect a unique life.
Q7: Do you make pet memorials?
Yes. We create custom pet memorials with the same craftsmanship and care as all of our memorials — because every life is precious and deserving of remembrance.
Q8: How do I get started?
Simply call us at (631) 727-4170, stop by our showroom at 129 Griffing Avenue in Riverhead, or use our contact form. We will guide you gently through every step of the process.
